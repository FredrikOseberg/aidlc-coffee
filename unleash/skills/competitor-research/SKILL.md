# Competitor research

Use WebSearch (and WebFetch for a specific page) to check what competing products offer. Your task names the product;
work out its competitors from that, or use the list in the project guidelines if there is one.

## Method

1. Start from specific questions ("Does the Starbucks UK app let you schedule pickup?"), not broad ones.
2. Keep it short: at most 6–8 searches for a whole scan. Prefer official sites, app-store listings and recent news.
3. For every claim, keep the source URL and the date if the page shows one. If you cannot confirm a claim, say "unconfirmed".
4. Summarise as a table: capability | which competitors have it | source.

## Interpreting

- **Parity gap**: most competitors have it and customers ask for it. Weighs heavily, because customers leave over it.
- **Differentiator**: few or none have it. Worth more only if customers clearly want it.
- **Noise**: a competitor launched it but nobody here is asking for it.
